Okayama International Circuit — Season Finale & Title Decider

18 June 2026 · 11 drivers · Two 17-lap sprints · Porsche 911 Cup (992.2) · Okayama (Full Course), Japan · Race 1 ↓ · Race 2 ↓ · CLS standings ↗

Champion of Season 4

🏆 Champion

Silvio Foth — #117, NJB Performance

Took the title fight by the throat: pole position, a lights-to-flag Race 1 win, and a battling P5 in the reverse-grid Race 2. With closest rival Andre Rajkovic scoring nothing all evening, Foth wraps up the Season 4 Porsche Community Cup crown.

Title Swing

Rajkovic’s double DNF

Championship leader on raw points but carrying a drop result, Andre Rajkovic (#99, Team Heusinkveld) disconnected from both races — out after four laps in Race 1, a no-score in Race 2 — and left the finale empty-handed.

Final Podium

Foth · Rajkovic · Brand

Going into the finale the net standings read Foth ahead of Rajkovic, with Willi Brand (#68) locked onto third. Foth’s finale haul confirms that order for the season. Official final points are confirmed once CLS publishes Round 8.

CAS PCCD · Season 4 · Round 8 · Race 2

Okayama — Race 2 (Feature, reverse-grid top 8)

18 June 2026 · 11 drivers · 17-lap sprint · Porsche 911 Cup (992.2) · Race 1 results ↓ · Finale summary ↑

By the Numbers

Race Winner

Benjamin Schlosser — P6 → P1

Reversed onto row three, Schlosser (#83) picked the five-car fight apart and drove away — five places gained, the race’s fastest lap (1:28.267) and a 12.7 s margin at the flag. A faultless drive to cap his strongest weekend of the season.

Battle of the Race

A five-car scrap for P2–P6

Thomas Felix, Andy Weber, Jan Tobias Schrader, Silvio Foth and Don Utz ran nose-to-tail for the entire second half. Just 2.7 seconds covered P2 to P6 at the line — trading places lap after lap while Schlosser escaped up front.

Contact & Karma

Foth, Brand & Utz tangle

The race’s flashpoint: Willi Brand ran wide and forced contact with Foth in the pack, then had to hand the place back. The three-way duel cost Brand dearly — he slipped from the fight to P7, while Foth salvaged P5.

Race Highlights

The reverse grid lit the fuse. Andy Weber led from reverse pole and Klaus Oberlaender slotted into second, but it never settled. A first-lap brush sent Oberlaender backwards, and from there a rolling five-car train formed for the minor places. Benjamin Schlosser, reversed only to P6, sliced through it inside a handful of laps and was gone — reeling off fastest lap after fastest lap out front.

Behind him, raw racing. Thomas Felix held firm to take a fine P2, Weber recovered to P3, and Schrader, Foth and Utz argued over the rest. The defining moment was the Foth–Brand–Utz tangle: Brand went wide, contact followed, and the stewards’ karma left him seventh. With Rajkovic absent, Foth’s P5 was more than enough to seal the championship. All ten runners were classified on the lead lap; Rajkovic did not take the restart.

CAS PCCD · Season 4 · Round 8 · Race 1

Okayama — Race 1 (Heat, standing start)

18 June 2026 · 11 drivers · 17-lap sprint · Porsche 911 Cup (992.2) · Race 2 results ↑ · Finale summary ↑

By the Numbers

Race Winner

Silvio Foth — pole to flag

The championship leader converted pole into a controlled lights-to-flag win, set the fastest lap (1:27.532) and crossed the line with a zero-incident sheet. Exactly the drive the title fight demanded.

Title Blow

Rajkovic out on lap 4

Andre Rajkovic ran in the leading group — and set a 1:27.730 — before disconnecting after four laps. The closest title rival scored nothing, a result Foth pounced on from the front.

Best of the Rest

Brand & Schlosser complete the podium

Willi Brand brought it home second at +2.2 s, with Benjamin Schlosser third — the same B. Schlosser whose P3 here reversed him to P6 for a winning Race 2.

Race Highlights

Foth made it look simple. Away cleanly from pole, he edged a small but steady gap on the tricky, technical Okayama layout while the chasers sorted themselves out behind. Willi Brand settled into second and Benjamin Schlosser into third, the leading trio quickly pulling clear of the midfield in identical Cup cars.

The drama was further back — and decisive. Okayama claimed several victims off its low-grip kerbs, but the moment that shaped the season came when Andre Rajkovic lost the car and then dropped out of the event entirely after four laps. With his nearest rival on zero, Foth’s lights-to-flag win turned a tight title race into a commanding lead. Behind the leaders, Schrader, Utz and Felix diced over the lower points places.

Hockenheim GP — Race 2 (Feature, reverse-grid top 8)

4 June 2026 · 12 drivers · 16-lap sprint · Porsche 911 Cup (992.2) · Race 1 results ↓ · Full race report ↗

2D telemetry replay — the ~26-minute sprint compressed to 60 seconds.

By the Numbers

Race Winner

Silvio Foth

From P6 on the reverse grid to the lead inside five laps — the decisive pass on Jan Tobias Schrader came with a slipstream run on the Parabolika. Controlled the gap to the flag and answered Andre Rajkovic’s Race 1 win in the title fight.

Photo Finish

Rajkovic vs Brand — same gap

P2 and P3 both classified at +3.02 s — Willi Brand closed onto Rajkovic’s gearbox on the final lap and crossed the line almost alongside, setting the race’s fastest lap (1:38.394) in the chase.

Charge of the Race

Benjamin Schlosser P10 → P4

Six positions gained from tenth on the grid, including a clean stab down the inside of the Sachskurve — the biggest climb of the evening. All 12 cars finished on the lead lap.

Race Highlights

The reverse grid delivered. Jan Tobias Schrader led confidently from reverse pole while the championship contenders carved forward from P6, P7 and P8. Foth needed only five laps to clear the field, with the decisive move a Parabolika slipstream run on Schrader; Rajkovic followed through to P2 soon after. Andy Weber’s race ended in spirit on lap 2 — a snap of oversteer from P2 dropped him to the back of the field.

The finish was the story: Brand erased a multi-second gap to Rajkovic over the closing laps — fastest lap of the race included — and the two crossed the line classified at the identical gap of +3.02 s. Behind them, Benjamin Schlosser’s six-place charge to P4 and Klaus Oberlaender’s outside pass on Schrader into the Motodrom (later undone by a half-spin) kept the midfield alive to the flag. All 12 cars finished on the lead lap — for the second time in one evening.

CAS PCCD · Season 4 · Round 7 · Race 1

Hockenheim GP — Race 1 (Heat, standing start)

4 June 2026 · 12 drivers · 16-lap sprint · Porsche 911 Cup (992.2) · Race 2 results ↑ · Full race report ↗

2D telemetry replay — the ~26-minute sprint compressed to 60 seconds.

By the Numbers

Race Winner

Andre Rajkovic

Took the lead from pole-sitter Willi Brand in the opening phase at the hairpin and defended it for 16 laps with just 2 incident points — by far the cleanest sheet in the lead group — final margin 0.50 s.

Fastest Lap

Silvio Foth — 1:38.458

Set on lap 11 while running P3 — over half a second quicker than his own qualifying time, and the fastest lap anyone managed all evening in either race.

Closest Podium

Top 3 within 1.02 s

After 26 minutes of racing, Rajkovic, Brand and Foth crossed the line covered by a second — and all 12 starters finished on the lead lap with zero retirements.

Data Views

Gap to Leader

The podium trio’s lines never separate — 16 laps inside roughly one second, the tightest lead group of the PCCD season.

Race Pace

Identical machinery, tight windows: the front four separated by consistency, not raw speed — B. Schlosser’s pace says podium, his finishing position says spin.

Pit Stops

Sprint format — no mandatory stop, and Race 1 stayed out of the pit lane almost entirely.

Incident Timeline

Every logged incident with exact race-time and type — including the early lead-group contact at the hairpin that decided the race.

Position Changes

Rajkovic’s P2→P1 swap with Brand around lap 5 and B. Schlosser’s drop out of the lead group on lap 7 are the two decisive moves; the rest of the field locks in early.

Net Overtaking

Battle Proximity

Laps spent within 1.5 s of one another — the Rajkovic–Brand–Foth triangle dominates the entire matrix.

Race Highlights

A 16-lap three-way fight. Pole-sitter Willi Brand defended hard through the opening laps, but a late-braking clash in the lead group at the hairpin handed Andre Rajkovic a lead he never relinquished — though he was never allowed more than about a second of breathing room. Benjamin Schlosser, a strong P3 qualifier, ran with the leaders until a solo spin on lap 7 dropped him down the order; he recovered to P10, which — via the reverse-grid rules — set up his Race 2 charge.

The data tells the closeness: nine of twelve drivers lapped in the 1:38s or low 1:39s, the podium finished within 1.02 s, and every single car completed all 16 laps. Silvio Foth’s 1:38.458 from P3 was the fastest lap anyone turned all night — pace that would pay off half an hour later.
