CAS IEC · Season 4 · Round 1

Sebring International Raceway — 3 Hours, Multiclass

5 September 2026 · 37 cars in three classes · 3-hour endurance · LMP2 · GT3 · Porsche 911 Cup · clear, 25 °C air / 31 °C track · Full race report ↗

2D telemetry replay — the full three hours compressed to 100 seconds, roughly 110× real time. Dot colour follows the car’s livery brand; the side panel is live running order and lap count.

By the Numbers

Overall & LMP2 Winner

WS Racing eSports LMP2 I — 92 laps

Daniel Langehegermann took overall pole with a 1:48.971 and the car led 52 of the 92 laps. He and Fabian Buhl finished 36.2 s clear on a combined three incident points — the cleanest front-running drive of the night.

The Other Two Classes

DaRoAn (GT3) · Aachen Yellow (Porsche Cup)

Andre N Villela won GT3 alone in the DaRoAn Acura by 30.3 s, on two incident points in three hours — the lowest figure of any car in the race. Kevin Küpper took the Porsche Cup by 30.2 s with the class’s fastest lap.

Team of the Day

Alemannia Aachen White — P13 → P2

Benjamin Höft drove all three hours on his own, started thirteenth of sixteen in LMP2 and finished second in class on the lead lap, leading four laps himself — on five incident points, against a class average above seventeen.

Fastest Lap

Martin Spieler — 1:49.021

The quickest lap of the race, set on lap 79 in the PWA E-Sports #BackFisch LMP2. Class bests: Michael Gasser 1:59.912 (GT3) and Kevin Küpper 2:01.469 (Porsche Cup).

🔴 The Race-Control Crash

One caution, and it came from a server

Race control and iRacing were on the same machine, and both crashed. On restart the software re-counted every car’s incidents and effectively doubled them — seven black flags in nine seconds, 24 more before the flag, and a Full Course Yellow that could not be cancelled. The one caution of the race, four laps long, was a software fault.

Attrition

Six cars gone, 248 logged incidents

Six cars disconnected or parked: Void Crown after two laps, Neon Pink after 41, Neon Orange after 45, KEK Racing after 56, Flying Arrow after 57, CAS-Tech Yellow after 20. Only five of sixteen LMP2 and four of sixteen GT3 reached the flag on the class lead lap.

Race Highlights

The season opener was decided as much by what broke as by what raced. WS Racing eSports led from overall pole and were never seriously threatened in LMP2, but almost everything behind them was shaped by attrition: six wheelbase failures counted across the field, a driver stuck in third gear handing his car over mid-stint, repeated disconnections, and a leading GT3 that threw away an hour’s work on track limits. In the Porsche Cup, Don Utz was wrecked while making room for a faster car and still came back out to take third in class, eight laps down.

Then race control itself became the story. With roughly a third of the race left, the machine running both iRacing and the race-control software crashed. On restart the software re-counted every car’s incidents and added them to what it already held, sending 31 black flags out in total — seven of them in nine seconds — and triggering a Full Course Yellow that could not be withdrawn. Some cars served drive-throughs they never owed; one owed a drive-through and never served it. Rather than rerun the race, the stewards reconstructed it from the official result, this telemetry recording, a full replay run and an independent breakdown, and applied a flat 13-second correction per unjustified drive-through. The result changed by exactly two positions.
