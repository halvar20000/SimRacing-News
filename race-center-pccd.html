Nürburgring Grand-Prix-Strecke — Race 2 (Feature, reverse-grid top 8)

30 July 2026 · 19 drivers · 13-lap sprint · Porsche 911 Cup (992.2) · dry, 25 °C track · Race 1 results ↓ · Full race report ↗

2D telemetry replay — the ~25-minute sprint compressed to 60 seconds.

By the Numbers

Race Winner

Maurice Becker — P8 to the double

Reversed to eighth, Becker was patient at the start and then simply quicker than everyone through the final chicane. He cleared Jan Tobias Schrader for the lead, pulled away and completed the perfect debut evening: two wins from two, by +4.55 s.

Driver of the Day

René Hollerung P18 → P7

Untouched by the top-8 inversion, Hollerung started 18th after his wrecked Heat and carved through to seventh — eleven places, eleven overtakes, the biggest gain of the night.

Fight for Second

Gründel × Grossenbacher — 0.48 s short

Three tenths a lap quicker in the closing stages, Gründel got right onto Grossenbacher’s gearbox but never found the move through the chicane. With the championship in mind he settled for third — the smart call.

Fastest Lap

Willi Brand — 1:57.054

Brand took the Feature’s quickest lap on his way to fourth, quicker than either of the drivers who beat him to the podium.

Best Defence

Schrader: reverse pole to P6, no mistakes

On pole from the inversion, Schrader lost the lead early but never binned it. His policy, explained afterwards: signal with a sharp weave and let the quicker cars through rather than lose time fighting them.

Attrition

Two disconnections, one black-flag start

Klaus Oberländer dropped out after a single lap and Clemens Müller-Rechner after five, both disconnected. Dominique Meyers drew a black-flag pass-through seconds after the green and served it on lap 1, yet still recovered to eighth.

Race Highlights

The inversion put Jan Tobias Schrader on pole and Becker back to eighth — and it barely mattered. Schrader lost the lead to Sean Pfennig almost immediately, Gründel was up to fourth before the end of lap 1, and Becker worked his way forward more deliberately before taking the lead and disappearing. The final chicane decided it: as the commentary noted, Becker and Gründel were taking it visibly quicker than anyone else, and that set up every pass they made. Two starts, two wins, on debut.

The best drive of the night came from the back. René Hollerung, outside the reversed top eight, started 18th and finished seventh — eleven places and eleven overtakes. Gründel closed to within half a second of Grossenbacher in the fight for second but chose the championship over the risk, and Willi Brand banked the fastest lap in fourth. Dominique Meyers served a black-flag pass-through on lap 1 and still recovered to eighth; Klaus Oberländer and Clemens Müller-Rechner both dropped out with disconnections, and Antonio Cursio’s 20 incident points and 2:39 repair left him a lap down.

Nürburgring Grand-Prix-Strecke — Race 1 (Heat, standing start)

30 July 2026 · 19 drivers · 13-lap sprint · Porsche 911 Cup (992.2) · dry, 25 °C track · Race 2 results ↑ · Full race report ↗

2D telemetry replay — the ~25-minute sprint compressed to 60 seconds.

By the Numbers

Race Winner

Maurice Becker — pole, win, fastest lap

On his very first PCCD start, Becker took pole by 0.003 s, lost the lead to Alex Gründel at Turn 1, spent ten laps shadowing him and finally struck in the Veedol chicane — then set the fastest lap (1:56.730) and won by +6.55 s.

Duel of the Race

Becker vs Gründel — ten laps nose-to-tail

The two 4000+ iRating men ran in a 0.18–0.52 s window for most of the race, the only close-battle pairing on the leaderboard. Hard, but as the commentary put it, „sehr, sehr fair“ — no contact all race.

Best of the Rest

Grossenbacher P6 → P3

Remo Grossenbacher climbed three places to the final podium step, ahead of Dominique Meyers and Willi Brand — who were split by just 0.003 s at the flag after 13 laps.

Driver of the Day

Benjamin Schlosser — recovery from two spins

Qualified fourth, Schlosser threw it away twice without outside help („ohne Fremdverschulden“), fell to P17 and clawed back to P12 with seven on-track overtakes — the highest Driver-of-the-Day score of the Heat.

Lap-1 Casualties

Utz, Coldron and Hollerung all pit

Something happened on the opening lap that the broadcast never resolved: three cars took repairs. Hollerung, winner of the Jerez Feature, lost 2:32 in the pits and was lapped by the leaders.

Toughest Run

Chistian Schlosser — 15 incident points

A 4:07 repair stop on lap 7 and a late black flag left him 19th, two laps down, with the highest incident count of the race.

Race Highlights

A debutant took over the Porsche Community Cup. Maurice Becker, running his first race of the season and carrying a ~5000 iRating, put it on pole by three thousandths of a second — then promptly lost the lead to Alex Gründel into the Arena at the start. What followed was the race: ten laps of Becker sitting between one and five tenths behind, probing the inside line at the end of the long straight, with neither car ever touching the other. The break came in the Veedol chicane, where a small Gründel error let Becker through. From there it was a 6.5-second win, with the fastest lap of the race thrown in on lap 9.

Behind the leaders, the opening lap did the damage. Benjamin Schlosser spun away fourth place unaided and then did it again later, dropping to P12; Don Utz, Max Coldron and René Hollerung all came in for repairs, Hollerung losing two and a half minutes and a lap. Remo Grossenbacher was the beneficiary, climbing from sixth to a podium, while Dominique Meyers and Willi Brand crossed the line three thousandths apart in fourth and fifth. Andre Brechmann and Klaus Oberländer had the closest scrap in the pack until Oberländer lost the rear under pressure through the Schumacher-S.

Circuito de Jerez — Race 2 (Feature, reverse-grid top 8)

16 July 2026 · 16 drivers · 15-lap sprint · Porsche 911 Cup (992.2) · Race 1 results ↓ · Full race report ↗

2D telemetry replay — the ~25-minute sprint compressed to 60 seconds.

By the Numbers

Race Winner

René Hollerung — reverse P3 to victory

Handed a front-row start by the reverse grid, Hollerung took control early, set the race’s fastest lap (1:41.718) and held a charging Alex Gründel at bay to win the Season 5 opener’s feature by just +1.09 s.

Charge of the Race

Gründel P8 → P2

The Heat winner, reversed all the way to eighth, sliced back to second and closed onto Hollerung’s gearbox at the flag — barely a second short of the double.

Driver of the Day

Antonio Cursio P16 → P8

Redemption after his Race 1 retirement: eight places gained from the very back, eight overtakes and a tidy 5-incident sheet — the cleanest recovery of the night.

Jump-Start Drama

Müller-Rechner loses reverse pole

Away too early from pole position, Clemens Müller-Rechner drew a black-flag pass-through, served it on lap 1 and tumbled from first to a recovered P10.

Attrition

Brechmann out after 7 laps

Andre Brechmann’s evening ended early — a spin and a technical issue left him parked, the only retirement of an otherwise full-distance field.

How Close

Top five in 3.75 s

Hollerung, Gründel, Barth, Grossenbacher and Brand were covered by under four seconds at the line — identical Cup cars, decided on track position.

Race Highlights

The reverse grid turned the order on its head. René Hollerung, dropped only to third by the top-8 inversion, was best placed of the genuine front-runners and used it — leading early, setting the fastest lap and managing the gap on a Jerez layout that offers precious few passing spots. From reverse pole, Clemens Müller-Rechner never got the chance: a jump-start drew a black-flag pass-through, served on the opening lap, and his race became a recovery drive to P10.

The story of the race was the chase. Alex Gründel, reversed to eighth after his Heat win, carved back to second and reeled Hollerung in over the final laps, crossing the line just +1.09 s adrift. Behind them Benedikt Barth, Remo Grossenbacher (up from P10) and Willi Brand completed a top five separated by under four seconds. Antonio Cursio took Driver-of-the-Day honours with a clean climb from last to eighth, while Carl Sören Schober’s combative night — a race-high 15 incidents and a late black flag — dropped him to P12. Andre Brechmann was the only retirement, parked after seven laps.

The day’s early casualty was Antonio Cursio, whose lap-2 repair stop dropped him a lap down to 16th; every other car finished on the lead lap, setting up the reverse-grid Feature.

CAS PCCD · Season 4 · Round 8 · Race 2

Okayama — Race 2 (Feature, reverse-grid top 8)

18 June 2026 · 11 drivers · 17-lap sprint · Porsche 911 Cup (992.2) · Race 1 results ↓ · Finale summary ↑

By the Numbers

Race Winner

Benjamin Schlosser — P6 → P1

Reversed onto row three, Schlosser (#83) picked the five-car fight apart and drove away — five places gained, the race’s fastest lap (1:28.267) and a 12.7 s margin at the flag. A faultless drive to cap his strongest weekend of the season.

Battle of the Race

A five-car scrap for P2–P6

Thomas Felix, Andy Weber, Jan Tobias Schrader, Silvio Foth and Don Utz ran nose-to-tail for the entire second half. Just 2.7 seconds covered P2 to P6 at the line — trading places lap after lap while Schlosser escaped up front.

Contact & Karma

Foth, Brand & Utz tangle

The race’s flashpoint: Willi Brand ran wide and forced contact with Foth in the pack, then had to hand the place back. The three-way duel cost Brand dearly — he slipped from the fight to P7, while Foth salvaged P5.

Race Highlights

The reverse grid lit the fuse. Andy Weber led from reverse pole and Klaus Oberlaender slotted into second, but it never settled. A first-lap brush sent Oberlaender backwards, and from there a rolling five-car train formed for the minor places. Benjamin Schlosser, reversed only to P6, sliced through it inside a handful of laps and was gone — reeling off fastest lap after fastest lap out front.
